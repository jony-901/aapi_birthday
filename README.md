# 🎂 Magical Birthday Surprise Website (Interactive Journey)

A luxury, interactive, animated birthday surprise web application with acoustic piano music, 3D gift box unboxing, birthday cake candle blowing, cake slicing, a heartfelt personalized letter, fireworks finale, and an instant **Surprise Creator Studio** with shareable URL links.

---

## 🌟 Stages & Features (100% English)

1. **🌌 Stage 1: Starry Cosmos & Passcode Lock**
   - Floating stars and glowing ambient cosmos background.
   - Passcode protection (`1234` default or custom PIN) with hint & skip options.
   - Smooth ascending melodic chime on unlock.

2. **🎁 Stage 2: 3D Animated Gift Box Unboxing**
   - 3D CSS perspective gift box with golden satin ribbon and 3D bow.
   - Interactive untie & lid flying animation with golden sparkle explosion.
   - Smooth acoustic piano "Happy Birthday" melody starts playing.

3. **🎂 Stage 3: Interactive Birthday Cake & Candle Blowing**
   - 3-tier birthday cake with glowing flickering candles.
   - **Blow Candles**: Tap button or blow directly into your microphone -> realistic smoke puff + "Make a Wish" toast!
   - **Cut Cake**: Animated knife slices the cake -> celebratory sparkles -> proceeds to the heartfelt letter.

4. **💌 Stage 4: Heartfelt Birthday Note & Fireworks Finale**
   - Pre-crafted, warm, emotional English birthday letter with typewriter streaming effect.
   - Fullscreen celebratory canvas fireworks in the background.
   - Replay journey and customize options.

---

## ⚙️ Easy Customizer & Shareable Link

- Click **⚙️ Customize** on the top navigation bar.
- Edit **Recipient Name**, **Sender Name**, and **Passcode**.
- Click **🔗 Generate Link** -> **📋 Copy Link** and send via WhatsApp, Messenger, or Email!
- All customized details are preserved directly inside the private link.
